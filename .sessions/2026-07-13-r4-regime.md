💡 **Session idea:** the per-split picks expose a confound the current
control-arm design cannot separate: the conditional arm searches 12
variants per train window and churned through 5–8 distinct picks over 10
splits per lane, while its control searches only 2 — so `control_arm_delta`
mixes "conditioning hurts" with "bigger grids overfit their train
windows". A cheap follow-up is a **cardinality-matched control**: pad the
control grid to the same 12 entries with performance-equivalent redundant
variants (or, cleaner, walk-forward each SINGLE conditional variant with
no selection and compare its stitched OOS to the matching control
directly, K counted at the full 12). If the deltas move toward zero under
matched cardinality, the round's two conditioning nulls (PR #92, this
slice) are partly a *selection-variance* result, not purely a
regime-information result — a different, and more general, lesson.
Differs from the r4-ensemble card's pairwise-committee idea (composition)
— this one is about making control arms selection-fair.
