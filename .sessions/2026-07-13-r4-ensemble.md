💡 **Session idea:** the diversification premium here has a SHAPE worth a
cheap follow-up: every KEEP is a small (2–3 member) committee of
closely-matched survivors, and every 5-, 6- and 9-member committee
KILLed against its own best member — equal-weight averaging over a wide
group dilutes toward the group mean, so the committee's fate is decided
by roster composition, not by combination skill. A follow-up slice could
test the *pairwise* frontier directly (all 2-member committees within
each group, K counted honestly at the full pair count) to separate "two
good complementary signals help" from "averaging many mediocre ones
hurts" — the current design confounds member count with member quality.
Differs from the r4-seasonality card's exposure-diagnostic idea (verdict
readability) — this one is about the next cheapest *composition*
hypothesis the committee result actually licenses.

## Why this session exists

Round 4 slice R4-C (`docs/research-round-4-plan.md` @ main, pre-registered
and merged before this session started): Round 3 never asked whether
survivors *combine*. Several instruments carry ≥2 KEEP-dev families from
different logic classes; this slice forms, per instrument×timeframe group
with ≥2 KEEP-dev lanes, an equal-weight signal committee — position =
mean of member positions — and grades it vs (a) the best single member
and (b) same-window same-cost B&H, on the same rail, costs
(5 bps + 1 bp), and walk-forward windows as the members. Members are
FROZEN as the lanes' already-committed walk-forward choices (no re-search
inside the committee — the frozen-replay precedent is PR #101). KEEP
universe: the **58** `new_verdict == "KEEP"` lanes of
`experiments/sweeps/r4-killsig-regrade/summary.json` (R4-A, PR #100).
Pre-registered rule: KEEP (dev-candidate only) iff the committee beats
BOTH the best single member and the benchmark on stitched OOS Sharpe;
otherwise KILL. t-stat informational only, at the round-standard K=12
(bar 2.638 — never lowered). Pre-registered hypothesis: diversification
raises Sharpe modestly, but no committee clears t ≥ 2.64. Owner mandate:
ORDER 012 generative rung; holdout SPENT; promotion CLOSED.
