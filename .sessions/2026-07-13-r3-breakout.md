💡 **Session idea:** every KEEP/KILL in this lane (and every prior sweep
lane) is cast on a stitched-OOS Sharpe whose ACTIVITY is never recorded —
the sweep records carry `oos_metrics.n_bars` but not how many trades or
how much time-in-market produced that Sharpe. For slow breakout families
this is the binding blind spot: an `atr_trailing` variant with a 55-bar
entry channel can sit flat for most of a 252-bar test window, so a lane's
stitched OOS Sharpe can rest on a handful of trades and near-zero
exposure, yet it is graded by the same rule as a family trading weekly —
"KEEP on 3 trades" and "KEEP on 60 trades" are currently
indistinguishable in the record. Adopt a per-lane OOS activity line:
have `walk_forward` also stitch the test-window POSITIONS it already
computes (it stitches returns/equity today; positions are in hand at the
same point), then record `oos_n_trades` (position-change pairs),
`oos_exposure` (mean position) alongside `oos_metrics`, and add a
first-class `LOW-ACTIVITY` qualifier on any verdict whose OOS trade
count is below a small pre-declared floor (e.g. 10) — a qualifier, not a
verdict change, so no existing KEEP/KILL flips retroactively. Test
targets: stitched positions align 1:1 with `oos_returns`' index,
`0 <= oos_exposure <= 1`, and the qualifier fires on a constructed
two-trade lane. Not covered by recent cards' ideas (cost-drag/breakeven
bps, sweep-verdicts index, sweep-runner extraction, control arms,
cache-provenance sidecar): those re-price or index numbers the lab keeps;
this records the sample-size dimension the verdicts silently rest on.
